Get started

Running in three steps.

  1. 1

    Install.

    Unzip and run AutoH-Setup-2.1.0.exe. Installs for your user only — no administrator rights.

  2. 2

    Make a mode, if you like.

    Choose apps and sites, give it a Ctrl+Alt hotkey. Voice works without any mode.

  3. 3

    Press it — or say it.

    Hit your hotkey, or hold Ctrl+Alt+V, say “เปิดไลน์” and let go.
